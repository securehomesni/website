An alarm should fit the way you live. On the survey we look at how you move around the house, where pets go, and which doors you use at night, then design a system that's simple to set and unset.

## What's included

- A free survey and a fixed written quote
- Wired or wireless systems, depending on your home and whether you're decorating
- Pet-tolerant sensors where needed
- An external bell box and an app to set, unset and check the alarm
- Installation to [PD 6662 and BS EN 50131] by SSAIB-registered engineers
- [Two-year] warranty on equipment and workmanship

## Monitoring options

You can keep things simple with a bells-only system, get alerts on your phone, or [add professional monitoring](/services/remote-monitoring/). We'll explain the cost and what each option does before you decide.

## Servicing

Alarms should be serviced [once a year] to keep insurers happy and batteries fresh. See [maintenance and servicing](/services/maintenance/).
